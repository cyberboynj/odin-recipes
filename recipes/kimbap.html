Kimbap

Description

Kimbap is a popular Korean dish made of rice and various fillings rolled in seaweed.

Ingredients

  • Cooked white rice
  • Seaweed sheets (nori)
  • Fillings (e.g., cooked beef, pickled radish, spinach , carrots, eggs)
  • Sesame oil
  • Salt

Instructions

  1. Season the cooked rice with sesame oil and salt. Mix well and let it cool to room temperature.
  2. Prepare the fillings by cooking the beef, sautéing the vegetables, and making a thin omelet with the eggs. Cut the omelet into strips.
  3. Place a seaweed sheet on a bamboo sushi mat. Spread a thin layer of the seasoned rice over the seaweed, leaving about an inch at the top edge.
  4. Arrange the fillings in a line across the middle of the rice.
  5. Using the bamboo mat, carefully roll the seaweed and rice over the fillings, applying gentle pressure to create a tight roll. Continue rolling until you reach the end of the seaweed sheet.
  6. Use a sharp knife to slice the kimbap into bite-sized pieces. Serve with soy sauce or a dipping sauce of your choice.
