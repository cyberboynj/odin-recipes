Ramen

Description

Ramen is a popular Japanese noodle soup.

Ingredients

  • Ramen noodles
  • Broth (chicken, pork, or vegetable)
  • Meat (chicken, pork, or beef)
  • Vegetables (green onions, mushrooms, etc.)
  • Soft-boiled egg
  • Soy sauce or miso paste

Instructions

  1. Cook the ramen noodles according to the package instructions. Drain and set aside.
  2. In a pot, heat the broth and add soy sauce or miso paste to taste. Bring to a simmer.
  3. In a separate pan, cook the meat until fully cooked. Set aside.
  4. Soft-boil the egg by boiling it for 6-7 minutes, then placing it in ice water before peeling.
  5. Assemble the ramen bowl by placing the noodles in the bottom, pouring the hot broth over them, and topping with meat, vegetables, and the soft-boiled egg.
  6. Serve hot and enjoy!
